# email_template.md — the shape of your outreach mail.

The agent adapts this to each post: it keeps the tone and structure, replaces the
bracketed parts with facts from the post and skills.md, and keeps the whole mail under
150 words. It never sends — it leaves the draft open in Gmail.

---
Subject: [Role from the post] — [your name], [one credential]

Hi [first name of the poster, or "team"],

[One sentence naming the exact role and where you saw it.]

[Two sentences: the two most relevant things from skills.md, with numbers.]

[One sentence tying it to something specific in the post or the company.]

I have attached my resume. Happy to share more or jump on a call this week.

Best,
[your name]
[phone] · [email] · [linkedin]
---

## Tone rules
- Plain text. No markdown, no bullet lists, no emoji.
- No "I hope this email finds you well", no "passionate", no "synergy".
- Never claim anything that is not in profile.md or skills.md.
